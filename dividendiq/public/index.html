Questions?

Frequently Asked

Is my portfolio data safe? ▼
Yes. Your data is stored securely in Supabase (SOC 2 certified infrastructure). We never sell your data and you can delete your account and all data at any time from your profile settings.
Which stocks are supported? ▼
All US-listed stocks and ETFs on NYSE and NASDAQ — thousands of tickers. Simply search by ticker symbol (e.g. KO, JNJ, AAPL) or company name.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription anytime? ▼
Absolutely. Cancel anytime from your profile — no questions asked. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.
How accurate is the dividend data? ▼
Real-time prices and dividend data come from Finnhub, a professional financial data provider. Forecasts are estimates based on historical dividend growth rates.
Is there a mobile app? ▼
DividendIQ is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — on mobile, tap "Add to Home Screen" in your browser to install it like a native app. It works on iOS and Android.
What's the difference between Free and Pro? ▼
Free gives you up to 10 positions, real-time prices, dividend calendar, and portfolio dashboard. Pro unlocks unlimited positions, full research access, price charts, dividend history, 25-year forecasts, watchlist, benchmark comparison, and no ads.
